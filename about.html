About me

Dependable support, built on the details.

I'm Michelle Pink Rejuso, a virtual assistant since August 2019, helping teams keep data, leads, and records accurate and organized.

I started in data entry and lead research, where I learned to check details, maintain clean records, and keep work moving without constant follow-up. I've spent years researching business contacts and organizing information in Google Sheets.

Before VA work, I worked in a BPO call center, handling inbound and outbound calls, follow-ups, and documentation. That experience shaped how I communicate with clients and customers today. More recently, I've transferred dental patient records from scanned forms into digital systems, with careful attention to accuracy and confidentiality.

For that same practice, I built The Hub, a web app the team now uses to run its daily operations. I've also started learning AI automation.

I value clear expectations, useful progress updates, and work that is ready for your team to use.

My goal is to take essential details off your plate so you can focus on the decisions that grow your business.

If this sounds like the support you need, get in touch.
